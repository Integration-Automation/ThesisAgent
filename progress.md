- **#11** A lightweight deck overflows its `meta` line when the paper title is long. Seen 2026-10-08 in the search smoke (`--query "transformer attention" --source arxiv --max 3`): `thesisagents review` reported `slide 3 / 4 / 5, shape "meta": overflows its box, rendered 0.6" vs 0.5"` for a 112-character title. The box is the title-plus-"Paper N of M" line in `_add_content_slide` (`thesisagents/exporters/pptx.py`), 0.5" tall at 15 pt with no shrink-to-fit, so a title that wraps to two lines does not fit. Not caused by the preflight work (`pptx.py` is unchanged there). Read `deck-design` and `slide-deck-rules` before changing the geometry.
- **#12** [UNVERIFIED] The `dblp` source returned a body that is not JSON. Seen once, 2026-10-08, in `--query "graph neural networks" --source arxiv,openalex,dblp,crossref,springer --max 5`: the new Sources table showed `dblp 0 returned  failed: [dblp] invalid JSON: Expecting value: line 1 column 1 (char 0)` while arXiv, OpenAlex and Crossref answered. Not known whether DBLP sent an error or rate-limit page (transient) or changed its response. Check what `thesisagents/sources/dblp/fetcher.py` receives (status code, content type) and whether a non-200 or an HTML body should map to `RateLimitError` / `SourceUnavailableError` instead of `ParseError`.
- **#15** A library keeps the first summary stored for a paper. `merge_papers` fills only empty fields, so a paper re-added with a better `PaperSummary` (a hand-authored rich one replacing an abstract-based one) keeps the old summary. - **#16** The research-question callout (`rq_box`) has a fixed height of 0.8 in, so a question that wraps to three lines overflows it. Seen 2026-10-08 while reviewing decks for U-20261008-07: an English `research_question` of about 170 characters gave `rq_box: overflows its box, rendered 0.95" vs 0.8"` from `scripts/check_overflow.py`, on the committed exporter as well, so it predates the template work. The stacked sections size their boxes from the estimated line count (`_stacked_body_height_in`), the callout in `_add_rq_callout` does not.
